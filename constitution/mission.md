# LIOS Mission

LIOS transforms human investment judgment into a version-controlled, AI-portable operating system.

## Core Purpose

Own exceptional businesses.
Construct an exceptional portfolio.
Maintain an exceptional operating system.

## Operating Model

LIOS separates permanent knowledge (constitution, locked decisions) from operational knowledge (portfolio state, market conditions) from research knowledge (company analysis). The repository is the permanent source of truth. AI agents are interchangeable execution engines.

The framework is modular, auditable, reproducible, extensible, and maintainable over decades. Investment intelligence flows through:

Constitution → Architecture → Research → Portfolio Construction → Execution → Continuous Review → Version Control
