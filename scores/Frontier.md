# Replacement Index Scores

Three-metric weighted score governing all Sleeve D surgery. Each metric 0-100.

| Metric | Weight |
|---|---|
| Frontier Score | 40% |
| Thesis Momentum | 35% |
| Diversification Value | 25% |

## Replacement Gate

Replacement approved ONLY when BOTH:
1. Candidate Replacement Index > Incumbent Replacement Index
2. At least TWO of:
   - Incumbent misses earnings or issues weak guidance
   - Incumbent thesis weakens materially
   - Candidate trades at substantially more attractive valuation
   - Candidate wave becomes significantly higher conviction

No replacement on: short-term price movement, unrealized loss, or excitement about a new name alone.
