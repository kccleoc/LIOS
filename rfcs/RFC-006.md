# RFC-006 — One defensive sleeve, measured by duration

**Status:** Accepted — implemented in v6.0.0
**Date:** 2026-11-16
**Amends:** RFC-005 (v5's four-sleeve structure), P1′, P2′, P4′, P6′, P8′
**Record:** ADR-008

---

## Proposal

Delete Sleeve Z. Merge cash, money market, bills and bond ETFs into a single
defensive sleeve C, which becomes the residual of the portfolio. Replace the
cash/bond split with one measurement inside C: average effective duration 1–6 years,
no single holding above 10 years.

## Why the split failed

| Defect in v5 | Cause |
|---|---|
| Two instruments holding the same exposure needed opposite classifications | The boundary was drawn by duration, and duration is a property of the instrument, not of its risk role |
| A 5% target with a 2.5pp tolerance | ±50% of the sleeve. Every deviation is "inside the band" or "a breach", with nothing in between |
| Two defensive targets that disagreed in the shipped state | Two independent numbers where one decision existed |
| A transition mandate whose only job was moving money between two defensive sleeves | A boundary that creates work rather than information |
| A soft breach carried for weeks at six times tolerance | A limit with no magnitude rule |

## Design

```
risky     = (A + B) / NLV
defensive = C / NLV        where C = NLV − A − B
```

- **One number per age band.** The glidepath sets risky weight and the B ceiling.
  A and C are both residual.
- **No internal distribution.** Cash and bonds are both C. Composition is a
  reporting line in Health Check §7, not an allocation rule.
- **The duration band is the control.** Average 1–6 years; no single holding above
  10. Measured from fact-sheet effective duration, value-weighted, with money
  duration reported in dollars.
- **Escalation.** The average is a quarterly style guard. A single holding above
  10 years is a hard breach (P7′). A soft breach beyond 3× tolerance, or one that
  survives two reviews, becomes hard.
- **Rotation inside C is not a transfer.** It needs no direction permission, because
  no sleeve weight changes.

## Rejected alternatives

1. **Widen Z's tolerance.** The target is the problem, not the tolerance.
2. **No constraint inside C.** Makes the defensive sleeve discretionary.
3. **A cash/bond sub-target inside C.** The v5 structure renamed.

## Implementation notes

- Constitution P1′ now names three sleeves; P6′ carries the duration band in place of
  the duration-sleeve target and the Z floor.
- Health Check drops from 15 sections to 14: the deleted sleeve's review merges into
  Sleeve C, which now reports composition, a duration table, money duration and band
  status.
- Validation gains Check 11 (C duration band). A check is the only thing that turns
  "defensive" from a word into a measurement.
- The state schema drops `sleeves.z`, adds `sleeves.c.duration_band`, and requires
  `duration_years` on every C position.
