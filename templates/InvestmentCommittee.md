## Decisions

A decision exists only if all six parts are recorded (Part V): the
candidate and the sleeve it enters; its written thesis (for B) or view
statement (for A) and the evidence; the dollar amount, the B ceiling
headroom it consumes, and the look-through it creates; the alternatives
considered — at least two — compared head-to-head with the candidate and
why each was rejected; the condition that would reverse it; and the
source of the dollars. Where the decision moves no money, the sixth part
is recorded as "no dollars moved". Benchmark performance must not appear
in any part.
