LIOS v6.0.1 — patch release from two independent external reviews of 6.0.0. The Top-up's standstill branch was deleted: the gaps over {A, C} always sum to the contribution, so "if sum(G+) = 0" could never run, and it was the branch that looked like it handled balance. No-borrowing is now a rule rather than an assumption — leverage <= 1.00x, buys only after their funding sells fill, rolled buys capped by available cash, and Check 12 aborts on negative cash. The duration band gained a value cap on long holdings (above 6 years, at most 50% of C) because an average plus a per-holding cap lets half the sleeve sit in a 10-year bond, and sales inside C are pro-rata so a crash response cannot spike the average mid-event. The US-situs stop-adding line now binds every operation, not only the Top-up, and names its cure. The sector basis is locked: changing it needs a version bump and an ADR, because a cap whose basis can be edited can be satisfied without trading. Also: risky_cap defined, negative A target floored and escalated, names_max made hard, escalation made symmetric with a sunset trigger and a 10-day IC deadline, a 0.5pp measurement buffer on hand-built caps, Check 5 recomputes from holdings, intra-C contributions buy pro-rata. Constitution P1'-P8'. Three sleeves A/B/C. Health Check 14 sections. Validation 12 checks. ADR-008, RFC-006.
