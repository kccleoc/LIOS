5.0.2
LIOS v5.0.2 — controls that described themselves instead of enforcing anything. Patch release, from an external adversarial review plus a value-level sweep of the private book. The validation pipeline could not pass on a post-mandate state (Checks 2/3/8 aborted on any not-yet-executed target) and one of its checks read a field v5 had deleted; a new Check 10 derives US-situs exposure from the position flags instead of trusting a hand-kept total. A soft breach now escalates to hard beyond 3x tolerance or after two consecutive reviews. Risky weight has a band and a trigger for the first time — the number the whole glidepath is built on was previously untested. The risky-cap fallback splits by gap rather than by target weight. The P4' direction table gains C->Z and A->A, because a Z-floor breach and a look-through breach inside A each triggered a Rebalance with no permitted direction. The grace-end de-risking step is rewritten: under contribution_tilt it demanded a sale that P4' prohibits. A skipped contribution becomes arrears rather than permanent drift, and an IC decision now requires five recorded items before Sleeve B can be funded. Constitution: P1'-P8'. Four sleeves A/B/C/Z. Risky weight is (A + B) / NLV. Health Check 15 sections, validation 10 checks. ADR-007, RFC-005.
