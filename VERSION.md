LIOS v5.0.1 — four declared controls that were never wired to anything. Patch release. The 25% sector cap now has a measured basis (GICS industry group, recorded in `constraints.sector_basis`) instead of a word nobody defined; broad-sector totals are a dashboard metric. The US-situs stop-adding line now binds the monthly Top-up, not just deliberate trades, because a waterfall that runs without a trigger is the only operation that can breach a hard cap by calendar. `names_max` and `position_cap_pct` are now enforced instead of declared. A holding at a 0% target is dispositioned at the next Rebalance — a residual-target formula can only allocate to Sleeve A as a whole, so such a holding otherwise had no exit path. Buy legs are sized on actual net proceeds, never on an estimate struck days before the fill. Constitution: P1′–P8′. Four sleeves A/B/C/Z. Risky weight is (A + B) / NLV. Health Check 15 sections.
