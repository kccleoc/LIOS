# ADR-008 — Merge the bond sleeve and the cash sleeve into one defensive sleeve

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-16
**Supersedes:** the four-sleeve structure of ADR-007 (P1′ in v5)
**Bumps:** v6.0.0 — a Locked Decision change, not a patch

---

## Context

v5 carried four sleeves: A conviction ETFs, B cap-only satellites, C bonds with
duration above three years, Z cash equivalents with a 5% target, a 3% floor and a
7.5% ceiling. The split was justified by risk role: duration risk versus none.

In practice the distinction did no work and generated most of v5's defects:

- Two instruments holding the same exposure had to be classified as one or the other,
  and one was misclassified for weeks.
- A 5% sleeve with a 2.5pp tolerance is **±50% of itself**. It is a suggestion
  dressed as a limit, and it breached continuously.
- The defensive block had two targets that could disagree — and in the shipped state
  they did.
- A single transition mandate existed to move money from one defensive sleeve to the
  other. A structure that needs a mandate to tidy itself is a structure with a
  boundary in the wrong place.
- All constituents settle in a day. There was no liquidity difference to preserve.

## Decision

Merge C and Z into **one defensive sleeve, C**, and:

1. make C the **residual** — `C = NLV − A − B` — so there is one defensive number per
   age band instead of two;
2. delete the cash target, the floor and the ceiling;
3. forbid an internal distribution: composition (cash vs bonds) is **reported, never
   targeted**;
4. replace the split with one measurement — the **duration band**: average effective
   duration 1–6 years, no single holding above 10 years;
5. treat rotation inside C as **not a transfer**, so curing the band needs no
   direction permission.

## Consequences

- Three sleeves, one denominator, two residuals (A and C). The glidepath carries one
  number per band.
- The failure mode the split was meant to prevent — the defensive sleeve becoming a
  duration bet — is now measured rather than implied by a name. That is the entire
  point: "defensive" is a role, and duration is the number.
- The lower bound forces the sleeve to hold some duration. A sleeve at 0.37 years is
  a cash balance earning nothing, which is a real cost and now a visible one.
- A barbell of bills plus one long bond can satisfy an average. The single-holding cap
  bounds it; the Health Check must name a barbell when it sees one.
- Fewer rules, fewer classifications, and one fewer place for a misclassification to
  hide.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Keep four sleeves, widen Z's tolerance.** Treats the symptom: the problem is that
  a 5% target cannot be struck, not that the tolerance is too tight.
- **Merge into C with no constraint at all.** Cleanest, and it makes the defensive
  sleeve fully discretionary — the one place where "I'll decide later" does not get
  decided. The duration band is one line; the alternative is a label.
- **Give C an explicit cash/bond sub-target.** That is the v5 structure under a new
  name, with the same two-target disagreement.
